# Georgia Gwinnett College program research

## Purpose and scope

This document records the Sprint 1 research review for PathFinder's Georgia
program recommendations. It compares Georgia Gwinnett College (GGC) program
descriptions with representative occupations in the U.S. Department of Labor's
O*NET system, then checks whether the occupational activities are consistent
with PathFinder's six traits.

The GGC degrees-and-programs page describes 19 majors. Its Bachelor's Degree
filter currently exposes the following 19 programs used in this review:

1. Biology
2. Business Administration
3. Chemistry
4. Cinema and Media Arts Production
5. Criminal Justice / Criminology
6. Education
7. English
8. Environmental Science
9. Exercise Science
10. Health Science
11. History
12. Human Services
13. Information Technology
14. Integrative Studies
15. Management Information Systems
16. Mathematics
17. Nursing
18. Political Science
19. Psychology

## Sources and method

GGC sources
- [Degrees and Programs](https://www.ggc.edu/academics/degrees-and-programs)

Additional research sources
- [Holland Codes / RIASEC through the O*NET Interest Profiler](https://www.onetcenter.org/IP.html)
- [O*NET occupations](https://www.onetonline.org/)

The comparison uses these PathFinder traits:

- `analytical`
- `investigative`
- `creative`
- `social`
- `handsOn`
- `leadership`

The primary and secondary labels below are **theory-informed PathFinder
profiles**, not official GGC classifications and not O*NET numerical scores.
